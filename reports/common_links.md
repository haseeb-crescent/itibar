# The common-link census

Generated by `make narrators` from the bundles. A common link is a partial common link (k = 2 qualifying
branches, README) with no partial common link above it; a group can have several. `out/narrators.parquet`
holds one row per narrator. Collection classes: **Ṣaḥīḥayn** = groups with a chain in al-Bukhārī or
Muslim; **ṣaḥīḥ class** = groups with a chain in a `books.tasnif_no = 1` book; **all** = every group.

- groups with chains: 20,282; (group, common link) pairs: 25,387; narrators who are a common link somewhere: 4,237
- share of common-link pairs at the companion level (Taqrīb level 1): 41.7%

Why the ranks fall where they do: a common link is the *uppermost* partial common link on its routes,
so a narrator who branches widely loses the label wherever someone above him also branches. For each
narrator, the groups where he is a partial common link but not a common link, and the common links
above him there (top 4):

- al-Zuhri: 786 such groups; above him: أبو هريرة الدوسي (r4396) 335; عائشة بنت أبي بكر الصديق (r4049) 203; أنس بن مالك الأنصاري (r720) 196; عبد الله بن العباس القرشي (r4883) 192
- Qatada: 508 such groups; above him: أنس بن مالك الأنصاري (r720) 211; أبو هريرة الدوسي (r4396) 206; عبد الله بن العباس القرشي (r4883) 135; عائشة بنت أبي بكر الصديق (r4049) 112
- al-A'mash: 617 such groups; above him: أبو هريرة الدوسي (r4396) 240; أنس بن مالك الأنصاري (r720) 157; عبد الله بن مسعود (r5079) 135; عبد الله بن العباس القرشي (r4883) 131
- Shu'ba: 1,033 such groups; above him: أبو هريرة الدوسي (r4396) 352; أنس بن مالك الأنصاري (r720) 268; عبد الله بن العباس القرشي (r4883) 224; عائشة بنت أبي بكر الصديق (r4049) 200
- Malik: 81 such groups; above him: أبو هريرة الدوسي (r4396) 28; عائشة بنت أبي بكر الصديق (r4049) 19; عبد الله بن عمر العدوي (r4967) 18; أنس بن مالك الأنصاري (r720) 16

Mālik is the exception: he is a partial common link in only 99 of his 1,155 on-path groups, because in 653 of them his node is also
the compiler of the *Muwaṭṭaʾ* (his own book sits in the group), and D14 makes a narrator acting as a
compiler a terminal: never a single strand, never a partial common link. The same holds for other
compiler-narrators (ʿAbd al-Razzāq: compiler role in 1,651 of 2,597 groups). Counting a compiler's narrator-role
branches is the alternative recorded in DECISIONS.md D51; it would change partial common links across
the corpus and is left for a later structure version.
