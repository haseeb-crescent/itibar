# The common-link census

Generated by `make narrators` from the bundles. A common link is a partial common link (k = 2 qualifying
branches, README) with no partial common link above it; a group can have several. `out/narrators.parquet`
holds one row per narrator. Collection classes: **Ṣaḥīḥayn** = groups with a chain in al-Bukhārī or
Muslim; **ṣaḥīḥ class** = groups with a chain in a `books.tasnif_no = 1` book; **all** = every group.

- groups with chains: 20,282; (group, common link) pairs: 21,408; narrators who are a common link somewhere: 3,962
- share of common-link pairs at the companion level (Taqrīb level 1): 51.1%

so a narrator who branches widely loses the label wherever someone above him also branches. For each
narrator, the groups where he is a partial common link but not a common link, and the common links
above him there (top 4):

Compiler roles are per edge (structure version 4, DECISIONS D61): a narrator who compiled a book in the group is a terminal
only on his edge into that book, and his edges as a teacher count like anyone's. Mālik is a partial common
link in 677 of his 1,155 on-path groups and the compiler of the *Muwaṭṭaʾ* in 653 of them;
his own book is one qualifying branch and each student who carries the report into another book is another.
Structure version 3 made his whole node terminal whenever the *Muwaṭṭaʾ* was in the group, which put him 131st (D51, superseded).

**Book transmitters** (`bundle.book_transmitters`, audit §11.5) are left out of these counts in the groups where they appear only in that role: عبد الله بن سابور البغوي (r23051) 220 groups; محمد بن يحيى الوراق (r30978) 25 groups.
