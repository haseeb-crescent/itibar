# The critics' verdicts: a test set from the texts

Generated by `make verdicts` (`itibar.verdicts`, `itibar.critics`) from `hadiths.nass` and the pipeline's outputs. Every claim is quoted from the hadith text it closes (`out/verdicts.parquet`: the span, a substring of `hadiths.nass`, gated); names are resolved among the narrators of that hadith's own chain(s); each claim is tested as it is stated — a relative claim as relative. **A failed claim is reported with what breaks it, never as the critic being wrong**: a route in a later book, a misresolved name and a route the critic did not accept are different findings and are kept apart.

## 4. Tafarrud: is the one the critic names a cut vertex of the routes through his teacher?
