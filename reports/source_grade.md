# What the recorded grade is: the source's grade, decoded

Generated by `make source-template` (`itibar.source_template`) from `sanads.hukum`, `sanads.matn_no`, `hadiths.matn_no` and `meaning_groups.matn_no`, all chains of the corpus. The decoded template is the v1 ruleset `source_template` (`config/structure.yaml: derive.source_template`); the pipeline applies it to every chain (`out/chains.parquet: code_source_template`) and group (`out/records.parquet`). Nothing here is fitted to itibar's own grades.

## 1. The sentences: template families

`sanads.hukum` holds 577,024 sentences, 44,524 distinct strings. Each is parsed into an opening (the code phrase), the narrators it names with the verdict word given for each («فيه X وهو L», «عدا X وهو L», «متهم بالوضع وهو X»), the break markers («لأن به موضع إرسال / تعليق / انقطاع بين X وY»), the «رجاله رجال الشيخين / البخاري / مسلم» clause and its exceptions («روى له البخاري تعليقًا / مقرونًا بغيره»). Replacing the names by {N} and the verdict words by {L} leaves 1,083 skeletons (`reports/source_templates.csv`, every one with its count and code distribution) in 978 families (list lengths dropped). 576,865 sentences (99.97%) parse completely; the rest are listed in §1.2.

**The narrators the sentence names include the template's weakest.** On the 310,476 chains whose sentence names narrators of the chain and whose recorded code the template reproduces, every position at the chain's weakest level is named in 97.81%; every narrator named is at the weakest level in 93.11% — the «عدا» lists name every narrator below the base wording («رجاله ثقات عدا X وهو صدوق ، وY وهو مقبول»), not only the weakest.
 A narrator the sentence names is among itibar's v1 weakest positions (book-role positions excluded) in 94.83% of them; the rest are chains whose weakest narrator is the compiler at pos 0, whom the template grades and itibar's v1 does not, or whose weakest itibar position is the unnamed person, whom the template does not grade.

- **The compiler at pos 0 is graded.** In the 5,528 single-chain hadith whose book-role position (the compiler at pos 0, or a book transmitter) is strictly the weakest narrator, the template code, which grades him, reproduces the record in 99.91%; graded without him (itibar's v1 reading) the template would miss most of them. `sanads.max_rank` includes pos 0 for the same reason (D21).
- **Breaks.** One break marker raises the code to at least 2, two or more to at least 3 («إسناد ضعيف لأن به موضع تعليق وموضع إرسال» is coded 3). The unnamed person (`اسم مبهم`) is not graded: a chain whose only weakness is an unnamed person is coded by its other narrators («إسناده متصل ، رجاله ثقات» over «عن رجل»). With an inqiṭāʿ or taʿlīq marker in the chain he is read as one more «موضع إرسال» (counted once with an irsal marker).
- **Undescribed narrators** (rank_no 0, the stubs) read «مجهول الحال» (level 7): «… عدا عبد المؤمن وهو مجهول الحال».
- **The companion slot** is read by rank_no like any position: a companion is level 1; «مختلف في صحبته», «لم تثبت له صحبة» take the level the source gives them.
- **Tadlīs** enters only as the verdict word: «صدوق إلا أنه يدلس», «صدوق مدلس» are level-4 labels and the chain is coded 1 whatever the transmission word; there is no tadlīs rule (a handful of hand-written sentences add «وفي الإسناد X متهم بالتدليس»).
- **«رجاله رجال الشيخين / البخاري / مسلم»** (40,620 single-chain sentences): the clause tracks `rawis.is_bukhari` / `is_muslim` over the chain below pos 0 (the clause «رجال الشيخين» present iff every such narrator is both: 86.48% of single-chain sentences with an opening that can carry it). Its exceptions «روى له البخاري تعليقًا» and «روى له البخاري مقرونًا بغيره» read information the dump does not carry: `is_bukhari` is 1 for a narrator al-Bukhārī cites only muʿallaq (Ḥammād b. Salama, Abū Dāwūd al-Ṭayālisī are 1 and 1).

## 3. The template as a v1 ruleset (`source_template`) and its reproduction

```
level      max rawis.rank_no over every position (the compiler at pos 0 included); an undescribed narrator
           (rank_no 0) reads «مجهول الحال», level 7; the unnamed person is not graded
code       grades.yaml code_source of that level: 1-3 -> 0, 4-6 -> 1, 7-9 -> 2, 10 -> 3, 11 -> 4, 12 -> 5
breaks     n = inqita' markers + ta'liq markers + 1 if (an irsal marker, or an unnamed person with an inqita' or
           ta'liq marker in the chain); n = 1 -> code >= 2, n >= 2 -> code >= 3
```

**Chain by chain**, the template code equals `sanads.matn_no` for 531,417 of 577,024 chains (**92.10%**). In hadith with one chain: 306,212 of 307,205 (**99.68%**); in hadith with several: 225,205 of 269,819 (83.47%).

**In hadith with several chains the sentences are attached to the wrong chains.** Compared as a multiset over the hadith's chains, the template codes equal the recorded codes in 574,847 chains' hadith (99.62% of all chains). Where they match as a multiset and not chain by chain, the recorded sentence often names a narrator who is in another chain of the same hadith and not in this one. The pairing is close to random:

| chains in the hadith | hadith whose chains' template codes all differ | recorded codes in sanad order | another pairing | no pairing | expected by chance in sanad order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 14,484 | 7,674 | 6,737 | 73 | 7,242 |
| 3 | 635 | 103 | 531 | 1 | 106 |

So `sanads.matn_no` and `sanads.hukum` describe a chain of the hadith, not necessarily the chain on their row. Codes read per hadith (the hadith grade, below) or per group are unaffected (a minimum does not depend on the pairing); anything read per chain — the residue's former `chain_choice` among them — is.

- `misattached` / `misattached_named`: the hadith's recorded codes are its chains' template codes, attached to other chains (with `_named`: the sentence names a narrator found in another chain of the hadith and not in this one).
- `label_changed`: a named narrator is in the chain but his `rawis.rank` label is no longer the word the sentence gives (the narrator table was edited after the sentence was written).
- `named_elsewhere` / `named_not_in_hadith`: the sentence names a narrator who is not in this chain (and the hadith's codes do not match as a multiset): the chain was re-resolved after grading.
- `empty_list`: «… لأن به موضع إرسال ، وفيه» with nothing after «وفيه»: the generator printed no narrator and kept code 2 whatever the narrators' levels.
- `maqbul_as_yuhsan`: a level-6 chain under «إسناده ضعيف ويحسن إذا توبع» (code 2) — a second wording for the maqbūl, without the narrator list, used beside «إسناده حسن في المتابعات والشواهد».
- `unnamed_as_weakness`: «إسناده ضعيف ، …» over an unnamed person in mid-chain (code 2): a wording that grades the unnamed person, which the main template does not.
