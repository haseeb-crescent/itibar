# The residue: where the derived grade and the recorded grade differ

Generated by `make residue` from `out/records.parquet` (ruleset `ibn_hajar`, derive version 4). Every group with chains whose recorded grade (`meaning_groups.matn_no`, codes 0–5) differs from the derived grade v2 is tagged by the mechanical cause the machinery can name, with the receipts stored per group (`residue_*` columns). Tags are not exclusive; the primary tag is the first in `residue.precedence` (chain_choice, conditional_narrator, tadlis_only, mursal_only, unnamed_top, unnamed_mid, corroboration, source_severe, boundary_0_1, level_only, unexplained). The report states what the chain shows and what it cannot see; it issues no verdict on which grade is right.

**5,774 of 20,282 groups disagree** (28.5%). Direction: *stricter* = our code higher (a weaker grade) than the source's; *lenient* = lower.

| direction | groups | share |
|---|---|---|
| lenient | 3,281 | 56.8% |
| stricter | 2,493 | 43.2% |

*Lenient* means the source records a grade below what the chain, read through the narrator labels and the corroboration rules, reaches. That is a statement about the mechanics of the two gradings — what the source's grader saw that the labels do not carry (the tags below) — not a statement that the source is too severe.

## 1. The unit of grading (`chain_choice`)

In **2,585 groups (44.8%)** another attested chain of the group yields exactly the source's code under the same ruleset: itibar grades the report by its best chain, the source may have graded a particular hadith. This is the one tag that is purely about the unit of grading. It is lenient by construction: the group's v2 chain is its lowest code, so another chain matching the source has a higher one.
