# The residue: where the derived grade and the recorded grade differ, in three layers

Generated by `make residue` from `out/records.parquet` (ruleset `ibn_hajar`, classical code map, derive version 5). PR 7 decoded the recorded grade ([reports/source_grade.md](source_grade.md)): a template reads each chain off the narrator table (the *template code*: levels 1–3 → 0, 4–6 → 1, 7–9 → 2, 10 → 3, 11 → 4, 12 → 5, every position graded, breaks raising the code), and the group's grade is its best chain's, with the editors' exceptions. So every group whose recorded grade differs from the derived grade v2 is decomposed exactly:

    recorded − v2  =  (recorded − R)     aggregation layer: the recorded group grade against its recorded best chain R
                   +  (R − ST)           chain layer: R against the template on itibar's chains (`source_template`, ST)
                   +  (ST − v2)          substance layer: the template against the classical ladder (v2)

A disagreeing group is attributed to the first layer that is not zero, so the three counts add up to the disagreement count (gated); `residue_layers` keeps every non-zero layer. Tags are mechanical and not exclusive; the primary tag is the first in `residue.precedence` among the layer's own tags (group_below_best_chain, group_above_best_chain, template_vs_record, code_map, book_role, break_rule, conditional_narrator, unnamed_top, unnamed_mid, undescribed, tadlis, mursal, corroboration, boundary_0_1, level_only, unexplained). The report states what the chains show and what they cannot; it issues no verdict on which grade is right.

**5,774 of 20,282 groups disagree** (28.5%). Direction: *stricter* = our code higher (a weaker grade) than the source's; *lenient* = lower.
