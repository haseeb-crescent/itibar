What itibar is, and why this PR exists
Every hadith comes with an isnad — the chain of named people who passed it down to the book that records it. Overlay every chain of one report across all books and you get a graph from the Prophet to the books: the isnad bundle. itibar builds that graph for every one of 20,745 reports in a 715k-hadith corpus (open data, CC-BY) and computes on it what the hadith sciences have always computed by hand: how many independent routes a report has (κ, at several narrator-grade thresholds), who all routes pass through (cut vertices, common links), and what grade the report mechanically earns from the critics' recorded verdicts on each narrator — with a receipt for every number. The outputs are a dataset, a paper, and a site. The standing rule: the machinery computes and reports; it never issues a verdict of its own, never uses "weak" or "fabricated" in outputs, and where the tradition has two readings both are implemented as selectable rulesets.
PR 4 (bucket 2) added the corroborated grade: a chain with a light defect (a narrator of poor memory, an unknown-status narrator, a gap at the top, a mudallis's ʿan) is repaired when an independent route avoids the defective narrator; a fatal defect (accused, liar) never is. It also added the classical number class (gharīb / ʿazīz / mashhūr / mutawātir), Ibn Hajar's qarāʾin flags, the join to al-Kattānī's mutawātir list, a common-link census per narrator, and a per-chapter census.
Review of PR 4 found six places where the mechanism departs from how the hadith sciences actually work, each visible in audit §11's disagreement table against the source's recorded grades. They are corrections of mechanism, not judgment, so they ship together before any grade number is quoted.
The task
Make six corrections to the bucket-2 machinery — attested witnesses only, tiered tadlīs with a hearing check, cross-group shawāhid, per-defect coverage, book-transmitter roles in the census, κ-based number class — plus a provenance note on the grade scale; remove the agreement gate; regenerate every report; keep all gold green.
Definition of done
1. A corroborating witness must be an attested chain
Today a witness may be a route spliced from several chains in the union graph (19.4% of upgrades, `witness_attested = false`). Splicing is correct for κ (connectivity) and wrong for corroboration: a mutābaʿa or shāhid is a narration by a real person recorded in a real chain. Change: a witness is a single attested chain (one `sanads` row) that avoids the positions to be covered and whose own chain grade is light-or-better (fatal-free). Drop the spliced fallback; keep a `witness_route_exists` flag for the record so the paper can say how often the graph had a route but no chain. Expect the block "ours 1 / source 3–5" (562 groups) to shrink; report the before/after.
2. Tadlīs has tiers; the ʿan is a defect only for some
The source has a binary `has_tadlis`. Ibn Hajar's Ṭabaqāt al-Mudallisīn (full title Taʿrīf Ahl al-Taqdīs bi-Marātib al-Mawṣūfīn bi-l-Tadlīs; on Shamela) sorts about 150 mudallisūn into five tiers. Classical practice: tiers 1 and 2 — their ʿan is accepted (tier 1 rarely did it; tier 2 are imams who did it rarely or only from reliable teachers); tier 3 — their ʿan is accepted only where explicit hearing is established; tiers 4 and 5 — rejected unless explicit hearing is shown.

* Digitize the book from Shamela into `lists/mudallisin.yaml`: tier, name as printed, Ibn Hajar's note, page. Match to `rawis` ids deterministically (name/nisba tokens, death year, tabaka); write a candidates file with scores; auto-accept unique high-score matches; leave the rest as `needs_review` rows for the user in `lists/mudallisin_confirmed.csv`. Record how many of the data's `has_tadlis = 1` narrators are in the book and how many are not (those keep the binary treatment, config `untiered_tadlis: tier_3`).
* Hearing check (`tasrih`): a muʿanʿan link teacher→student by a tier-3 mudallis is covered if the same (teacher, student) link appears with an explicit hearing word (ḥaddathanā / ḥaddathanī / akhbaranā / samiʿtu / anbaʾanā …, the existing `words.py` classes) in any chain of the same report (`tasrih_scope: group`, default) or anywhere in the corpus (`tasrih_scope: corpus`, variant, closer to "thabata samāʿuhu minhu"). Covered links are not defects.
* Ṣaḥīḥayn convention (`sahihayn_muanan_accepted: true`, default): a mudallis's ʿan in a chain of al-Bukhārī or Muslim is carried on established hearing. Quote the classical statement for this in the README.
* Tier 1–2 ʿan: no defect. Tier 3: defect unless covered. Tier 4–5: defect, and additionally the chain cannot rise above ḍaʿīf by corroboration of that link alone (config). Report how the 271 "ʿan-only" downgrades from PR 4 redistribute.

3. Shawāhid live in sibling groups
A shāhid is another companion's report of the same meaning. The source files many such reports as separate meaning groups (al-Tirmidhī's «وفي الباب» reports for the sandals dowry, group 2031, are the proven case). Add `shahid_scope: group | siblings` (default `siblings`): when no in-group witness exists, search attested chains in the other meaning groups under the same leaf topic node(s) (`topics` nested set), requiring token-overlap between the candidate's matn and the chain's matn ≥ `shahid_min_overlap` (config; start at the lists matcher's normalization; pick the floor by inspecting 30 cross-group witnesses and record the inspection). A cross-group witness is always classed `shahid` and flagged `cross_group = true` with the sibling group id in the receipt. Report agreement with and without. The sandals entry's `classical_claim_holds_in_group` becomes a second assertion: holds with siblings.
4. Cover every light defect
Replace `avoid: weakest` as default with `avoid: each_defect`: for every light-defect position in the chain (and every structural light defect), there must exist a witness (per §1) avoiding that position; witnesses may differ per defect. Keep `weakest` and `all_light_defects` (one witness avoiding all) as variants with their numbers. This is the reading of «متى توبع السيئ الحفظ بمعتبر» applied to each defective narrator; say so in the README.
Remove the agreement gate (v2 ≥ v1). Agreement with the source's recorded grades is a reported result in audit §11, never a test, because a gate that rewards agreement invites tuning (D37 records exactly that pressure). Replace it with rule-consistency gates on synthetic bundles and the gold cases only.
5. Book transmitters and compiler roles in the census

* Compiler role per edge, not per node. A narrator who compiled a book is a terminal only on the edge into his own compiler node; his edges as a teacher elsewhere count for single-strand, partial-common-link and common-link detection like anyone else's. (Today the whole node is terminal when his book is in the group, which pushes Mālik to 131st and ʿAbd al-Razzāq similarly.)
* Book transmitters. Detect, per book, a narrator who occupies position 0 in a large share of the book's chains while the book's author sits at position 1 (al-Dabarī for ʿAbd al-Razzāq's Muṣannaf; ʿAbdallāh b. Aḥmad for the Musnad where applicable). Give him role `book_transmitter` for that book: exempt from grade thresholds and terminal on those edges, like a compiler, and excluded from the census counts in that role. Audit table: every book with such a narrator, his share, and whether `book_compilers` currently misidentifies him as the compiler; fix `book_compilers` where it does.
* Regenerate `reports/common_links.md`. State in the report where Mālik, al-Zuhrī, Qatāda, al-Aʿmash and Shuʿba now rank, and remove the D51 explanation if it no longer applies.

6. Number class on κ
Make `number_class` κ-based (κ at `all`: 1 gharīb, 2 ʿazīz, 3 to N−1 mashhūr, ≥ N mutawātir_count; N = `tawatur_n` = 10), because tafarrud is a node property — one man through whom all routes pass — and that is a cut vertex. Keep the width-based class as `number_class_width` (diagnostic; D43 documents why it over-reads gharīb on this tabaqa scale). `gharib_mutlaq` vs `gharib_nisbi` from whether the cut vertex is a companion. Update the gold number-class assertions accordingly; they must still pass (innamā scoped: gharīb mutlaq with cut vertex ʿUmar; lā yuʾminu scoped: ʿazīz; man kadhaba: mutawātir_count).
7. Provenance of the grade scale
The label vocabulary («صدوق حسن الحديث» on 5,649 narrators, and the level set) is the formula of Taḥrīr al-Taqrīb (Bashshār ʿAwwād Maʿrūf and Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ, 1997), a critical revision of Ibn Hajar's Taqrīb, and the source grades 49,844 narrators where the Taqrīb covers roughly 8,800. Sample 40 narrators present in the Taqrīb (Shamela) and compare the data's label with Ibn Hajar's and with Taḥrīr's; report the counts. README and the limitations text then say precisely whose verdicts these are: Taḥrīr's revision for Six-Books narrators (if the sample confirms), the source's own editors for the rest. Store `in_taqrib` per narrator if a reliable membership test exists (e.g. the Taqrīb's narrator index on Shamela); otherwise record that it does not.
8. The unknown-person residue
626 of the disagreeing groups have the unnamed person (`اسم مبهم`) as the weakest. Analyse them: position of the mubham (top vs mid-chain), the source's grade, whether another chain of the group names the person at that position (the classical way a mubham is identified). Add `unknown_repair: none | named_elsewhere` (default `named_elsewhere`: a mid-chain mubham is treated as covered when another attested chain of the same report has a named narrator at that position between the same teacher and student). Record the effect.

Decisions already made

* Witnesses are attested chains. Tadlīs is tiered with a hearing check at group scope by default. Shāhid search extends to sibling groups by default with an overlap floor. Per-defect coverage is the default. Compiler terminal-ness is per edge; book transmitters are a role. Number class is κ-based. The agreement gate is removed and agreement is reported only. Nothing is tuned to raise agreement.
* Human review rows (`lists/mudallisin_confirmed.csv`, `lists/confirmed.csv`) are written only by the user.

Out of scope
The text layer (alignment, variants, congruence) beyond the token-overlap floor used for sibling shawāhid; the site; a name-resolution model.
