itibar — PR 6: the unnamed companion, conditional narrators, and the residue
Read `README.md`, `DECISIONS.md` (D1–D68) and `docs/prompts/bucket-1.md`, `bucket-2.md`, `pr-5.md` before writing code; they define every term used below.
What itibar is, and why this PR exists
Every hadith comes with an isnad — the chain of named people who passed it down to the book that records it. Overlay every chain of one report across all books and you get a graph from the Prophet to the books: the isnad bundle. itibar builds that graph for every one of 20,745 reports in a 715k-hadith corpus (hadith-kg, CC-BY) and computes on it what the hadith sciences have always computed by hand: how many independent routes a report has (κ, at several narrator-grade thresholds), who all routes pass through (cut vertices, common links), the classical number class (gharīb / ʿazīz / mashhūr / mutawātir), and what grade the report mechanically earns from the critics' recorded verdicts on each narrator, with and without corroboration — with a receipt for every number. The outputs are a dataset, a paper, and a site. The standing rule: the machinery computes and reports; it never issues a verdict of its own, never uses "weak" or "fabricated" in outputs, and where the tradition has two readings both are implemented as selectable rulesets. Nothing is tuned to raise agreement with anyone's grades.
PR 5 made the corroborated grade (v2) classically exact: witnesses are attested chains, tadlīs is tiered from Ibn Hajar's Ṭabaqāt al-Mudallisīn with a hearing check, every light defect needs its own witness, the number class is κ-based, compiler roles are per edge. The agreement of v2 with the source's recorded grade (`meaning_groups.matn_no`) sits at 71.2% (`ibn_hajar`), 72.1% (`ibn_al_salah`), v1 72.4%. The number did not move across PR 4 → 5 because the corrections pull both ways; where it should have moved it did: groups whose weakest narrator is Taqrīb level 5 or 6 went from 16%/13% agreement to 48%/45%. What remains — the residue, audit §11.9 — is now legible, and its narrator table is a list of a specific kind of narrator: the unnamed person (631 disagreeing groups), then Ibn Isḥāq, Ibn Lahīʿa, al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī, Baqiyya, Abū Isḥāq al-Sabīʿī, Simāk b. Ḥarb, Ḥajjāj b. Arṭāt, Ibn Jurayj, al-Layth b. Abī Sulaym, ʿAlī b. Zayd b. Judʿān, Ismāʿīl b. ʿAyyāsh. Every one of these is a conditional narrator: his standing depends on whom he narrates from, through whom he is narrated, or when he was heard — Ibn Lahīʿa through the ʿAbādila, Ismāʿīl b. ʿAyyāsh from Syrians, Simāk from ʿIkrima, Abū Isḥāq before his ikhtilāṭ, Baqiyya and Ibn Isḥāq only with explicit hearing. A single Taqrīb level cannot carry that, and the chain-only machinery therefore cannot see it, while every grader does. That is the first thing this PR adds. The second is the single largest residue item: an unnamed person in the companion slot is today read as mursal, although the tradition accepts «رجل من أصحاب النبي ﷺ» as a companion whose anonymity does no harm. The third is the residue itself, decomposed by cause with receipts, which is section 5 of the paper. Two defaults from PR 5 are also changed, for reasons recorded below.
The task
Add conditional narrator rulings and the unnamed companion to the machinery; decompose the remaining disagreement with the source's grades by mechanical cause; flip two defaults; regenerate every report; keep all gold green.
Definition of done

1. The unnamed companion (structure)

Today every chain whose top narrator is the unnamed-person placeholder (`rawis.id` 1131, «اسم مبهم»; 5,040 chain tops, D36 `top_mubham: mursal`) is read as mursal and gets no PROPHET edge. The tradition distinguishes two cases. When a Successor says «عن رجل من أصحاب النبي ﷺ» the chain is connected and sound as far as that link goes, because the companions are all accepted and not knowing which one does not harm (Ibn al-Ṣalāḥ, Muqaddima, in the discussion of the mursal and of the majhūl; al-Khaṭīb, al-Kifāya; Ibn Hajar, al-Nukat ʿalā Ibn al-Ṣalāḥ and Nuzhat al-Naẓar on the mubham — find the sentences, verify them against the Shamela page text and quote them in the README). When he says only «عن رجل» or «عن شيخ», the man may be a Successor and the chain stays as it is today.

* Recover the mention text of the unnamed person at the top of each chain: `hadith_rawis` gives [start, end) offsets of each narrator mention in `hadiths.nass`; `words.py` already aligns chains to mentions (exact for single-chain hadith, in-order subsequence for taḥwīl). Where alignment fails, fall back to the sanad part of the text (before `matn_start`) and record the fallback in the receipt. Tabulate every distinct mention string at a chain top with its count before deciding anything, and put the table in the audit.
* Classify by a phrase list in `config/structure.yaml` (`unnamed_companion.phrases`), each phrase with its class: `companion` for designations of the Prophet's community — «من أصحاب النبي», «من أصحاب رسول الله», «من الصحابة», «صحابي», «صحابية», «ممن صحب النبي», «رأى النبي», «من أهل بدر»; `ansar_muhajir` for «من المهاجرين», «من الأنصار» when the man narrates directly from the Prophet (accepted as companion by default, counted separately so the variant `ansar_muhajir: mursal` can flip it); `none` for bare «رجل», «شيخ», «امرأة», tribal and regional designations («من بني فلان», «من أهل البصرة»), «بعض أصحابنا», and kinship («أبيه», «عمه» — these are name resolution, out of scope). Anything not in the list is `none` and counted as `unclassified` in the audit so the list can be completed by the reviewer.
* An unnamed companion is a companion: a PROPHET edge, Taqrīb level 1, no defect in v2. In κ, all unnamed companions of one group are merged into a single node `u<group_id>` (one per group, whatever the student), because distinctness must be demonstrable and two «رجل من أصحاب النبي» may be one man; the unmerged count is stored as `kappa_unnamed_split` for the record. The merged node is never a common link in the census (`narrators.py` skips it) and is reported as the cut vertex where it is one. The number class follows κ as before.
* Record the effect: chain tops reclassified by class; groups whose κ_all moves from 0 (`kappa_zero_reason: no_companion_end`) to ≥ 1; the number-class redistribution; the mursal count; v2 before/after on the 631 unnamed-person disagreements (top vs mid). Keep `top_mubham: mursal` as a variant. `structure.version: 5`.

2. Conditional narrators (derive)

A narrator's standing can be conditional on the link, not the man. The tradition records four observable kinds and some unobservable ones:

* `from_shaykh` — weak or sound from a named teacher only. Simāk b. Ḥarb from ʿIkrima («وروايته عن عكرمة خاصة مضطربة»); Sufyān b. Ḥusayn from al-Zuhrī; Maʿmar from Thābit, al-Aʿmash and Hishām b. ʿUrwa; Jarīr b. Ḥāzim from Qatāda; Ḥammād b. Salama from Qays b. Saʿd; Zuhayr b. Muʿāwiya from Abū Isḥāq (late); and the ten narrators whose `rawis.rank` label already carries «إلا في روايته عن …» / «تكلم في حديثه عن …» (audit Appendix A). Ibn Rajab's Sharḥ ʿIlal al-Tirmidhī has the systematic list (the sections on trustworthy narrators weakened in some of their teachers, in narrators of a region, and in a period of their lives).
* `region_of_shaykh` — Ismāʿīl b. ʿAyyāsh («صدوق في روايته عن أهل بلده مخلط في غيرهم»), Baqiyya in part, and whoever else the sources name. The teacher's region: `rawis` has no region column, so take it from the nisba tokens of his name against a list in the YAML (الشامي، الحمصي، الدمشقي، الحلبي …) and say so in the receipt; where the sources enumerate the Syrian teachers by name, use the names.
* `via_student` — sound only through named students. Ibn Lahīʿa through the ʿAbādila — ʿAbdallāh b. al-Mubārak, ʿAbdallāh b. Wahb, ʿAbdallāh b. Yazīd al-Muqriʾ, (ʿAbdallāh b. Maslama al-Qaʿnabī per some) — the Taqrīb entry itself says their narration from him is more acceptable; verify the wording.
* `ikhtilat` — the narrator's memory failed late in life and what was heard after is not accepted; what was heard before is; what cannot be placed is treated as after (Ibn al-Ṣalāḥ, Muqaddima, the type on those who became confused late: «يُقبل حديث من أخذ عنهم قبل الاختلاط ولا يُقبل حديث من أخذ عنهم بعد الاختلاط أو أشكل أمره» — verify and quote). The ruling is on the student → narrator link: `before: [student ids]`, `after: [student ids]`, `unknown: after`. The defect is light — Ibn Hajar's Nuzha names «المختلط الذي لم يتميز» among those whose hadith becomes ḥasan by corroboration, in the same sentence as the poor-memory narrator and the mastūr — verify the sentence. Ṣaḥīḥayn convention: what al-Bukhārī and Muslim took from a mukhtaliṭ is carried as heard before (find the classical statement; Ibn al-Ṣalāḥ discusses Saʿīd b. Abī ʿArūba and ʿAbd al-Razzāq in exactly these terms), `sahihayn_ikhtilat_before: true`. Sources for the student lists: al-ʿAlāʾī's al-Mukhtaliṭīn, Sibṭ Ibn al-ʿAjamī's al-Ightibāṭ bi-man rumiya min al-ruwāt bi-l-ikhtilāṭ, Ibn al-Kayyāl's al-Kawākib al-Nayyirāt, plus the Taqrīb/Taḥrīr entries; the 43 narrators whose label carries «اختلط» / «تغير بأخرة» (Appendix A) are the seed.
* `unobservable` — conditions the chain cannot show: from his book vs from memory, heard in Baghdad vs Basra («من سمع منه ببغداد فبعد الاختلاط» can sometimes be mapped to students; otherwise it is unobservable), after his books burned. Record these rulings in the table with `observable: false` and apply nothing; they are reported in the residue (§3) as "what the chain cannot see".

Build:

* `lists/conditional.yaml`, generated from `lists/conditional_confirmed.csv` by the same pattern as the mudallisīn (D57): one row per (narrator id, kind, condition ids, effect, source book, page, quoted sentence). Effects: `level: N` (the link takes Taqrīb level N in place of the narrator's), or `defect: light` (a light defect at that position, repairable like any other), or `accepted` (no defect where the base label would give one). Seed set, all of it: the fifteen narrators in audit §11.9's disagreement table; every narrator whose `rawis.rank` label carries a condition (Appendix A: «إلا في», «روايته عن», «اختلط», «تغير», «بأخرة», «أهل بلده»); every entry of the ikhtilāṭ books above; every narrator in Ibn Rajab's sections. For each, fetch the Taqrīb (Shamela 8609) and Taḥrīr (127715) entries with the provenance client and the relevant page of Ibn Rajab / al-ʿAlāʾī / Sibṭ Ibn al-ʿAjamī (search Shamela by title, record the ids in `lists/source/`). Match names to `rawis` ids with the deterministic matcher of `mudallisin.py` (same auto rules: score and margin, ism + father agreeing); the condition's teachers and students are matched the same way, restricted to narrators actually adjacent to him in the bundles (which removes most ambiguity — list the candidates from `sanad_rawis`, not from all of `rawis`). Auto-confirm a row when the Taqrīb or Taḥrīr entry of the narrator himself states the condition in words and every id in it matched uniquely; everything else is a `needs_review` row for the user. Human rows are the user's and are never written by the pipeline.
* Apply in `corroborate.py` on the link, before the chain grade: the position's effective level is the ruling's where its condition holds on that link, the narrator's otherwise; the receipt names the ruling, the source sentence, and which ids satisfied the condition. The chain grade stays weakest-link. κ thresholds are node-level and are not changed by link rulings; say so in the README. `conditional: true` default, `conditional: false` variant.
* Tier-4 mudallisūn with hearing elsewhere: Ibn Hajar's tier 4 is accepted «بما صرحوا فيه بالسماع»; PR 5 applied the hearing-elsewhere check (`tasrih_scope: group`) to tier 3 only. Extend it to tier 4: a tier-4 ʿan covered by a hearing word on the same teacher → student link in another chain of the report is no defect (Ibn Isḥāq's «حدثني» in a parallel chain is the common case); uncovered it stays as D57 has it. Report how the 98 Ibn Isḥāq and the Baqiyya disagreements move.
* `derive.version: 4`. Count: narrators with rulings, rulings by kind, links where a ruling fired, groups whose v2 changed, and the disagreement table before/after. Nothing in the table may be derived from the source's grades; every row cites a book.

3. The residue report

`reports/residue.md` and `figures/fig6_residue.*`: every group where v2 (`ibn_hajar`) disagrees with `matn_no`, tagged by mechanical cause with receipts. Tags are not exclusive; all are stored per group in `out/records.parquet`; a primary tag is picked by a precedence recorded in `config/structure.yaml` (`residue.precedence`). Direction first: `stricter` (our code higher than the source's) or `lenient` (lower) — today 61% of disagreements are lenient, i.e. the source grades below what the chain alone warrants, which is the claim the paper needs to make carefully.

* `chain_choice` — another attested chain of the group yields exactly the source's code under the same ruleset (we grade the group by its best chain; the source may have graded a particular hadith). Report its share first; it is the one tag that is purely about the unit of grading.
* `source_severe` — source code 3–5 while every narrator of our v2 chain is level ≤ 9: the grader's verdict is harsher than any narrator's label allows, so it rests on the text or on a narrator verdict not in the labels.
* `conditional_narrator` — a weakest-position narrator has a ruling in §2; sub-tags `resolved` (the grades now agree), `unobservable` (his only ruling is unobservable), `unresolved`.
* `tadlis_only`, `mursal_only`, `unnamed_top`, `unnamed_mid` — the chain's only defects are of that kind.
* `corroboration` — v2 upgraded by a witness and the source did not follow, or v2 found no witness and the source is higher; record the witness's divergence class.
* `boundary_0_1` — both codes in {0, 1}: the ṣaḥīḥ/ḥasan line. For the 100 narrators most often weakest in these groups, fetch Ibn Hajar's and Taḥrīr's verdicts (the provenance client) and tag `tahrir_vs_ibn_hajar` where the two differ in a direction that explains the disagreement (the labels follow Taḥrīr's formula, §11.7; the grades may follow Ibn Hajar's).
* `level_only` — no defect beyond the levels and no corroboration: a plain weakest-link mismatch, i.e. the grader's narrator verdict differs from the label or an ʿilla is in play.
* `unexplained` — none of the above.

Tables: counts by primary tag × direction; by weakest level; by collection class (Ṣaḥīḥayn / ṣaḥīḥ class / rest); by level-1 chapter; the narrators most often weakest under each tag; the unobservable conditions and the groups they touch; a deterministic sample of 50 disagreeing groups (seeded) printed with their full receipts for the reader. The report states what the chain cannot see — matn-based verdicts, ʿilal, pair-specific rulings not yet in §2, narrator verdicts other than the labels — as a list with the counts that fall under each, and nothing else: no verdict on who is right.

Decisions already made

* An unnamed person designated as a companion is a companion; bare «رجل» stays mursal; unnamed companions merge per group in κ.
* Conditional rulings are link-level, cite a book, and never touch κ thresholds. Unobservable conditions are recorded and applied nowhere.
* The hearing-elsewhere check covers tiers 3 and 4.
* Sibling shawāhid are a variant; the mudallis set is the Ṭabaqāt union.
* Agreement is reported, never gated, never targeted. The residue report explains disagreement by mechanism and issues no verdict.
* Human review rows (`lists/conditional_confirmed.csv`, `lists/mudallisin_confirmed.csv`, `lists/confirmed.csv`) are written only by the user.

Out of scope
The text layer (alignment, variants, meaning-based shāhid detection, the 216 unmatched Kattānī entries); name resolution of «أبيه» / «عمه» and homonyms; the site; the paper's prose.
How to work
Verify each rule against the classical text before encoding it and quote it. Build the ruling table from the books, not from the disagreement table: the disagreeing narrators are the seed for where to look, never evidence for what the ruling is. Apply the changes in the order above and record the disagreement table after each, so the audit shows which change moved what. Where the data or sources present a case these definitions do not settle, choose, record it in DECISIONS with the alternative, and keep going.
